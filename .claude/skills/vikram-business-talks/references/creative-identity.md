## 1. Script DNA

### Language & level
- Simple Telugu in English letters (Tenglish). **[STATED]**
- A 10th-class student must understand complicated business topics. **[STATED]**
- English business words that Telugu speakers already use stay in English (profit, loss, market,
  investment, startup, share, revenue). Explain anything heavier in Telugu right after the word,
  the first time it appears: *"Valuation ante — aa company ki market lo entha viluva undi ani
  investors anukuntunnaro adhi."* **[DEFAULT]**

### Voice
- Must sound like Vikram naturally explaining to his audience, not an AI article or a news report. **[STATED]**
- Talk *to* the viewer: "manam", "mee", "meeku telusa?", "ippudu oka vishayam chudandi". **[DEFAULT]**
- Short spoken sentences. One idea per sentence. Mix short punchy lines with medium explanation lines. **[DEFAULT]**

### Flow
- Continuous flow. No disconnected points, robotic headings, repetitive explanations or
  bullet-style narration. Each sentence connects to the previous one. **[STATED]**
- Connective tissue to use: *"Ikkade asalu twist undi…", "Ante enti?", "Simple ga cheppalante…",
  "Kani ikkada oka problem undi…", "Idi ardham avvalante mundu… telusukovali", "Andukane…",
  "Ippudu asalu question enti ante…"*. Vary them and never use the same one twice in a row. **[DEFAULT]**
- Each paragraph ends on an open question or a tension that the next paragraph answers
  (curiosity chain). **[DEFAULT]**

### Structure (spoken, never shown as headings)
1. **Hook (0–15s).** A surprising fact, number or contradiction about something the viewer uses daily. **[GAP: Vikram's real hook pattern]**
2. **Stakes.** Why this matters to *the viewer's* money, job or daily life. **[DEFAULT]**
3. **Background.** How it started, told as a story with people and dates. **[DEFAULT]**
4. **Mechanism.** How the business model actually makes or loses money, with one simple analogy
   (kirana shop, tea stall, auto driver, family budget). **[DEFAULT]**
5. **Turn.** The twist, problem or hidden side. **[DEFAULT]**
6. **Perspectives.** At least two sides (company / critics / customers / investors), stated fairly. **[STATED: "different perspectives"]**
7. **Lesson.** What the viewer can learn or do. **[DEFAULT]**
8. **Close + CTA.** **[GAP: Vikram's real closing line / CTA wording]**. Until confirmed, use a single
   natural line asking a question for the comments + subscribe, never a long list of asks. **[DEFAULT]**

### Numbers, money, business models
- Explain every number so it means something: compare, scale down, or relate it
  ("roju ki ~₹X crore", "mana state budget lo sagam"). **[DEFAULT]**
- Never stack more than 2 numbers in one sentence. **[DEFAULT]**
- Pronunciation rules: `numbers-pronunciation.md`.

### Facts & perspectives
- Accuracy is mandatory for business/finance. **[STATED]**
- Say where a fact comes from in the narration when it is surprising or contested
  ("company annual report prakaram…", "RBI data chuste…"). **[DEFAULT]**
- Allegations are framed as allegations ("ani aaropanalu unnayi"), never as proven fact. **[DEFAULT]**

## 2. Editing DNA

### Mix & references
- ~40% relevant realistic footage + ~60% custom animation and motion graphics. **[STATED]**
- Visual references: Vikram's **TBT** and **Hospital Expose** videos. **[STATED]**
  Their specific techniques are **[GAP]**.
- Real photographs, cinematic footage, carefully designed compositions, professional motion graphics. **[STATED]**
- Every visual communicates the exact meaning of the narration. **[STATED]**

### Banned
- Random stock video, unnecessary emojis, decorative symbols, boring black screens with headings,
  cheap animations, generic templates, artificial AI-looking visuals. **[STATED]**

### Footage (the 40%)
- Must be specific to the subject: the actual company, founder, product, store, city, news clip or
  document. "Generic businessman shaking hands" fails. **[DEFAULT, derived from STATED ban]**
- Slow cinematic push-ins (Ken Burns) on still photos; never leave a still static >2s. **[DEFAULT]**

### Motion graphics (the 60%)
- Data visualisation for every key number: animated counters, bar/line charts that build, maps with
  routes, money-flow diagrams (customer → company → supplier), timelines, org/ownership trees,
  document/headline highlights (paper texture + underline/marker reveal). **[DEFAULT]**
- One idea per frame. Animate *in step with* the word that names it. **[DEFAULT]**
- Website / product promotion: real screen recording or clean UI mock in a device frame, with
  cursor/scroll motion and callouts on the exact feature mentioned. **[DEFAULT; specifics GAP]**

### Typography & text
- Brand wordmark: "VIKRAM" in heavy geometric sans, white, caps; "BUSINESS TALKS" smaller, gold,
  wide letter-spacing, between thin gold rules. **[OBSERVED]**
- On-screen keyword text: max ~5 words, highlight the key number or word in gold. **[DEFAULT]**
- Font family: **[GAP]**. The observed wordmark matches a Montserrat/Poppins-style geometric sans.

### Captions
- Tenglish, synced word-groups of 2–5 words, lower-middle safe area, key word highlighted gold. **[DEFAULT; style GAP]**

### Colour
- Brand palette **[OBSERVED]**: deep navy background (~`#0B1530`→`#101C3D` gradient), gold/amber
  accent (~`#F2B934` / `#D9A42A`), white primary text, red only for the Subscribe button (`#E5332A`).
- Subtle grid/star texture and faint rising-chart lines in backgrounds. **[OBSERVED]**
- Footage grading: **[GAP]**. Default: natural, slightly cool shadows / warm highlights so footage

### Camera & transitions
- Cinematic zooms are part of the style. **[STATED]** Exact pacing is **[GAP]**.
- Transitions motivated by content (match-cut, whip on topic change, graphic wipe in brand gold).
  No random flashy presets. **[DEFAULT]**

### Sound

## 4. Audience retention techniques **[DEFAULT unless noted]**
- Open loop in the first 15s that is paid off late in the video.
- A new visual every 2–4s; a new "reason to keep watching" every 45–60s.
- Pattern interrupts through visuals (graphic ↔ footage switch), not cheap noises.
- No filler intros like "Hello friends, welcome back" before the hook. **[GAP: confirm Vikram's intro]**
