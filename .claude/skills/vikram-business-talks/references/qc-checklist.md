# VBT Final QC Checklist

## Script
- [ ] Is it VBT tone, with nothing from the psychology channel?
- [ ] Is it Tenglish throughout, and would a 10th-class student understand every line?
- [ ] Is there no heading, bullet, numbered list or "point 1/2/3" inside the narration?
- [ ] Does every sentence connect to the previous one? (connection test, W4)
- [ ] Is there no repeated explanation?
- [ ] Is there no article/news/AI phrasing ("ee video lo manam chuddam", "conclusion ga", "mukhyamaina amsham")?
- [ ] Does the hook land in the first 15s with a specific fact or contradiction?
- [ ] Does every paragraph end with tension/a question, and is the open loop paid off?
- [ ] Are at least two perspectives presented fairly?
- [ ] Is the close natural, with a single clear CTA?

## Facts
- [ ] Is every number, date and name in the W1 facts table with a source and as-of date?
- [ ] Is no ✗-unverified claim stated as fact? Are allegations framed as allegations?
- [ ] Spoken figure = on-screen figure = source figure?
- [ ] Are Indian numbering and the ₹ conversions correct?

## Edit plan
- [ ] Is the footage/animation ratio ≈ 40/60, with the actual % reported?
- [ ] Does every beat's visual show the exact meaning of its line?
- [ ] Is it free of random stock, emojis, decorative symbols, black-screen headings and template/AI-looking visuals?
- [ ] Does no beat stay static for more than ~6s, and does no still sit without motion for more than 2s?
- [ ] Is the brand palette (navy / gold / white) used consistently?
- [ ] Are music changes at the act turns, with SFX only on real reveals?
- [ ] Does every footage item have a specific source + licence note?

## Captions
- [ ] Do the captions use the exact narration words, 2–5 words each, with no split numbers, and are they synced?

## Packaging
- [ ] Does the thumbnail have ≤ 3 elements and ≤ 4 words, stay readable at mobile size, follow the brand palette and avoid AI-plastic looks?
- [ ] Do the title and thumbnail complement each other, and does the video pay off the promise?
- [ ] Does the description include chapters + sources + handle?
